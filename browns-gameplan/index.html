Preparation and sources

Pick a formation and a run/pass check before the snap. Check the box and perimeter numbers. Bubble calls need favorable perimeter leverage.

On passes, know the primary read and one outlet. Move on when the first window closes. Use Green’s speed when a bootleg or open running lane gives you space. TE/slot seams and Seattle are planned downfield calls.

After a run loss, use the new down-and-distance menu. Compare Inside Zone, HB Mid Zone and HB Zone WK in practice. Promote the runs that hold up against your opponents. This sheet does not assume a run is proven.

QB runs and options are a regular part of the plan. Practice stock-call motion timing, RPO reads and choice routes. Protect the ball with Green. In two-minute offense, account for the clock on runs and underneath completions.

Each situation has 10–15 calls across 3–5 formations. Formations are alphabetical. Types follow the legend; preferred calls lead within each type. All 67 favorites remain in the reference list.
