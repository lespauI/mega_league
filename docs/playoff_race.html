How the probabilities are calculated

The report simulates the remaining season 10,000 times. Team strength is 70% performance win percentage and 30% past schedule strength, excluding Force Wins. A team's match win probability is its strength divided by the combined strengths, bounded to 25–75%. Official results and the existing tiebreakers determine playoff seeds.

MAKE SUPER BOWL means reaching the Super Bowl, not winning it. Each conference has seven playoff teams and one first-round bye for seed #1, followed by reseeding. In playoff games, home victory odds are multiplied by 10^(75/400), approximately 1.54 (the equivalent of +75 ELO); the resulting probability is also bounded to 25–75%. The simulation stops at the conference finals. Appearance probabilities sum to 100% in AFC and 100% in NFC before rounding.
