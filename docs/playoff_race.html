How the probabilities are calculated

The report uses 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations of the remaining schedule. Game probabilities combine the supplied ELO ratings, current win percentage, schedule strength and strength of victory, with the existing home-field, streak and divisional adjustments. The existing tiebreakers determine the playoff teams in each simulation.

The tables above show the current export's results. Formula reference · Export and ELO provenance

Методология: SOS (Strength of Schedule) рассчитывается на основе рейтингов соперников по атаке, защите и общей производительности.

Высокий SOS = Тяжёлое расписание | Низкий SOS = Лёгкий путь в плей-офф
